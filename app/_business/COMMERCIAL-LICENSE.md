## 1. Who needs a commercial licence

The PolyForm Shield License allows any use of the software except one: providing a product that
competes with the software, or with any product G&G Technologies S.r.l. provides using it. A
commercial licence is needed for that use, and only for that use. For example:

- selling, renting or giving away an application that does the same job as the software, modified
  or not, under any name;
- offering the software, or a product built on it, as a hosted or managed service, with or without
  a backend of one's own;
- bundling the software into a larger product that is offered as a substitute for it — a suite, an
  ERP, a practice-management system with an invoicing, planning or questionnaire module taken from
  it;
- distributing a branded or white-label edition to the customers or members of an organisation.

Products compete even when they are free of charge, written in another language or offered on
another platform, as the PolyForm Shield License states.

## 2. Who does not

No commercial licence and no payment are needed to:

- use the software, changed or not, for the work of one's own company, including commercial work;
- read, study, change and share the code, keeping the licence and the `Required Notice:` lines;
- build on it a product that does a different job;
- provide consulting, installation or support to someone who uses the software under the PolyForm
  Shield License.

When in doubt, a written question to the address in section 8 receives a written answer.

## 3. What a commercial licence grants

Within the scope set in the agreement, a commercial licence grants the licensee a non-exclusive,
non-transferable right to:

- provide the product named in the agreement, including uses that section 1 lists;
- change the software and combine it with other works for that product;
- distribute the software, changed or not, to the licensee's customers as part of that product, or
  make it available to them as a service;
- keep the licensee's own changes and additions private.

The agreement names the product, the territory if any, the number of end users or installations if
any, and the versions covered.

## 4. What it requires

- **Attribution.** Every copy of the software, or of any part of it, keeps the `Required Notice:`
  lines of the `NOTICE` file. The product also names G&G Technologies S.r.l. as the author of the
  software in a place where its users can see it — an "About" screen, the credits, or the
  documentation — unless the agreement sets a different form.
- **No sublicensing.** The licensee may not grant anyone else the right to provide a competing
  product. End users of the licensee's product receive only the right to use it.
- **Marks.** The licence covers copyright and patents. It grants no right to the names Plan Scope,
  Invoice Scope, Survey Scope or G&G Technologies, nor to the marks and logos of G&G Technologies,
  except to state truthfully that the product is based on the software.

## 5. Fees and term

Fees are set in the agreement. G&G Technologies S.r.l. offers two forms, and may agree others:

- **annual**: the right lasts for the year paid for and covers every version released in that
  year; it renews by payment;
- **perpetual**: a one-off fee for the versions released up to a date set in the agreement; later
  versions need a new agreement or an annual renewal.

Fees are exclusive of VAT and other taxes where applicable.

## 6. Termination

If the licensee breaches the agreement and does not remedy the breach within 30 days of written
notice, G&G Technologies S.r.l. may terminate it. On termination the licensee stops providing the
competing product; end users keep what they had already received, unless the agreement says
otherwise.

## 7. Warranty and liability

The software is provided as it is. Any support, maintenance, warranty or service level is set in
the agreement and exists only there. As far as the law allows, the liability of G&G Technologies
S.r.l. under a commercial licence is limited to the fees paid for it in the twelve months before the
claim.
