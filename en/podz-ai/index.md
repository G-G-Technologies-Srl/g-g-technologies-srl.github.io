*Three design choices*

## Local by default. Masked before it leaves. Specialists you install.

### Local-first and private

Conversations and documents stay on your machine, in an encrypted database, in a folder that remains yours.

- The account is optional
- Encrypted local database
- Works offline with the local engine

### Built-in anonymiser

If a job goes through the cloud, personal data can be masked before it is sent and restored in the answer.

- Masking of names, dates and addresses
- Masked fields are replaced before the request leaves your machine
- The user decides what may leave

### One-click specialists

Expertise you install like an app: Legal Assistant, Web Researcher, CV Screening.

- You write to Podz.AI, it picks the specialist
- Podz.AI brings in the right one for the question
- They install and update one at a time
