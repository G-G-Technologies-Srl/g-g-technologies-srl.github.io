*About us*

## A team that keeps electronics, robotics and AI together.

**G&G Technologies Srl** designs and builds technology from the Republic of San Marino. It is led by **Gian Angelo Geminiani**, who has worked in this trade since 1997. Around him, a network of collaborators and technology partners across several countries: software architects, electronics designers, system engineers, chosen for the project in hand. It is how a small firm keeps electronics, robotics and AI together: one skill at a time, picked for the project.

This group of people has digitalised sales processes, automated factories and designed wearable solutions for vital-signs monitoring in healthcare and sport — most of it before the name on the door existed, and from inside those companies rather than as suppliers. It all sits on our **DigiSense®** framework, and that is what we built **Podz.AI** on: the personal AI workstation that brings artificial intelligence to the computers of people who work with confidential data.

The wearable work went into two [peer-reviewed studies](https://ggtechnologies.sm/en/services/medical-wearables/) in “Sensors”. The 2024 one, written with the Italian National Research Council and four universities, followed nine hundred emergency workers for twelve months.

We work side by side with entrepreneurs and professionals: first we understand the process, then we choose the technology. Never the other way around.

*Where we come from*

## The trade starts in 1997.

From factory processes to wearable devices, from algorithms to university research. In chronological order.

1997 – 2002

### Silcea Group, then SkyLab

Head of information systems at Silcea Group, tasked with putting the new ERP into production — the system that holds production, stock and accounts together. Then the founding of SkyLab, which assembled workstations for architecture practices, with graphics cards chosen to run CAD. Sizing a machine around the load it has to carry is the same trade as today, when the question is how much GPU memory it takes to keep a model in-house.

2002 – 2020

### Eighteen years at Gruppo Fontanot

From 2002 project manager and software architect at Albini & Fontanot Informatica, then CIO, then head of development and digital innovation manager for the group. Production processes, logistics, sales network management, product configurators. Among the things designed, machine learning in production before it was called AI. The first dates from the early 2000s and watched over a mobile-racking warehouse — the kind that runs on rails and opens one aisle at a time: before the aisle closed again, a camera checked it was clear, recognising people and obstructions in shed light that shifts from morning to evening. Later, machine learning applied to quality control.

2007 – 2008

### Research at Food Science, University of Bologna

A traceability platform for fresh-cut fruit and vegetables: RFID smart labels read on the products, and a modular algorithm that walks back up the supply chain from any document, showing each party — consumer, control authority, producer — only what concerns them.

2015 – 2024

### Botika, applied artificial intelligence

Geminiani co-founded and led Botika — which started as FunnyGain — a company that brings algorithms into industrial processes. In 2019 Confindustria Romagna picked it as the first San Marino technology partner in its SmartTech team.

2020 – 2024

### Chief technology officer at AccYouRate Group

Almost five years as CTO of a company that designs, patents and manufactures wearable devices for vital-signs monitoring. It is one of the affiliations on the [study published in “Sensors”](https://ggtechnologies.sm/en/services/medical-wearables/).

from 2022
