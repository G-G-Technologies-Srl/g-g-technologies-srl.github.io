# Privacy notice

*Privacy*

Which personal data G&G Technologies processes, for what purposes and for how long, and how to exercise your rights.

## Browsing the website

The website consists of static pages hosted by GitHub Pages, a GitHub service. It contains no forms to fill in, no analytics tools, no tracking pixels, no advertising and no content embedded from other sites, such as videos or maps. The typefaces, too, are those already installed on the reader's device: reading the pages sends no requests to third-party services.

As with any hosting service, GitHub logs the IP address of visitors for security purposes, together with the technical data of the request. G&G Technologies does not receive these logs and does not use them. The processing is described in the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Cookies and local storage

The website **uses no cookies**, neither technical nor profiling ones, and therefore shows no consent banner.

A single item is kept in the browser, through the feature called *localStorage*: the preference between the light and the dark theme, saved under the name *gg-theme* when the visitor chooses it with the theme button. The item stays in the browser, is not sent to G&G Technologies or to anyone else, and serves only to display the pages in the chosen theme. As storage strictly necessary for a feature requested by the visitor, it requires no consent. It is removed from the browser settings, by deleting the site data.

## Free apps

The apps published in the [Apps](https://ggtechnologies.sm/en/app/) section run entirely in the browser. The data entered — projects, invoices, questionnaires, opened files — remain on the device of the person using them: in the browser storage or in the folders that person chooses to connect. G&G Technologies does not receive these data and cannot access them.

Once loaded, the only request an app sends is the periodic reload of its own update file from the ggtechnologies.sm website, to check whether a new version exists. The request carries no personal data and is logged by GitHub like any other visit. App data are removed from the browser settings, by deleting the site data.

## Enquiries

Anyone who writes to the email address or to the certified email address, or telephones, provides the data needed to receive a reply: usually a name, an email address or telephone number, an organisation and the content of the enquiry. These data are used to reply and, if requested, to prepare a quotation. Without them the enquiry cannot be followed up.

If no business relationship follows, messages are kept for 12 months from the last exchange and then deleted.

Company email is managed by a hosting provider with servers in the European Union, which processes the data on behalf of G&G Technologies.

## Clients and course participants

For clients, including those enrolled on training courses, the data needed for the relationship are processed: identification and billing data, contact details, payment references and correspondence. The purposes are the performance of the contract, invoicing and compliance with accounting and tax obligations. Billing data are kept for the period required by tax and civil law; correspondence for the duration of the relationship and, afterwards, until the rights arising from it are time-barred.

Online courses are held on Google Meet. Taking part requires the link sent by email and a name to appear under; Google processes the connection data according to its own [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Sessions are not recorded.

During the courses each participant works with their own accounts, for example Claude, GitHub or Google, which remain theirs: G&G Technologies does not receive their credentials and does not access their content.

The courses are open to adults only.

## Recipients of the data

The data are disclosed only to the parties needed for the purposes described:

- the provider of company email;
- GitHub, for hosting the website;
- Google, for the online course sessions;
- the professionals who assist G&G Technologies with accounting and tax compliance;
- banks, for handling payments;
- public authorities, where the law requires it.

The data are not sold or passed on to third parties for commercial purposes, and are not used to send promotional messages.

## Transfers outside the European Union

GitHub and Google may process data in the United States. Both participate in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, the framework recognised by the European Commission for transfers to the US organisations that take part in it. GitHub also states that it relies on the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

## Automated decisions

No processing involves decisions based solely on automated processing, nor any profiling of individuals.

## Your rights

At any time you may request, under Articles 15 to 22 of Law no. 171/2018 and of the GDPR:

- access to your data and a copy of them;
- rectification of inaccurate data or completion of incomplete data;
- erasure;
- restriction of processing;
- portability of the data you have provided to us, in a commonly used format;
- objection to processing based on legitimate interest.

Requests are sent to [info@ggtechnologies.sm](mailto:info@ggtechnologies.sm) or to the certified email address [ggtechnologies@pec-legal.it](mailto:ggtechnologies@pec-legal.it). A reply is given within one month of the request; in the cases provided for by law this period may be extended.
