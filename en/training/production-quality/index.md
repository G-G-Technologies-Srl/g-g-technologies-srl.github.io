*The course*

## The shop-floor data is already there. The course teaches how to read it.

Machine exports, logs and department sheets hold information that is rarely read in full. The course shows how to organise it, analyse it and turn it into a repeatable report.

**Who it is for.** Production, quality and maintenance managers.

#### Duration

4 hours, in two two-hour sessions.

#### Format

Online or on site, at least 5 people.

#### Tools

Claude Pro or ChatGPT Plus. The course runs entirely in the browser.

#### Fee

From €100 per person, VAT excluded; on site plus €800. Paid in advance. [All fees](https://ggtechnologies.sm/en/training/#fees).

*The programme*

## Two sessions, each with a result ready for use.

### First session · Analysing the data

Exports from machines and management systems, logs and department sheets: organising and analysing them with the assistant.

- Cleaning and organising CSV files
- Checking the results of the analysis
- **Result:** A repeatable analysis of a production file

### Second session · Reports and alerts

From data to the shift or weekly report, with the source of each value stated alongside it.

- Alert thresholds
- A reusable report template
- **Result:** A report template and the alert rules

### At the end

The materials produced in the two sessions are exported to the participant's own files, which open in any program and can be used independently.

- A repeatable analysis of a production file
- A report template and the alert rules
- The course materials

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need data from my own company?

No. The exercises use production files provided in the session; participants who wish can apply the method to their own data within the rules of the foundation course.

### Do I need to know a programming language?

No. The course uses the assistant in the browser, without writing code.

By writing to info@ggtechnologies.sm with the format and the number of participants. Enrolment is completed by paying in advance; courses start with at least 5 people.
