The main result

Your website, online and on your own domain.

Home, services, about, contact and blog, with your design and your copy, published on GitHub Pages. If you have a domain, the website answers at your address, over HTTPS. Code, copy and images are yours.

The brand book

Colours, typefaces, mark and tone of voice, written as rules every role follows.

The group of agents

The four skills installed in your Claude account and the project's instruction file.

The engine and the checks

SEO in place

Statistics

The blog under way

The working group

Four roles working on the website, one person who decides.

Each role is a Claude skill: written instructions the agent follows in every session. They remain yours and improve over time, because a correction requested twice becomes a rule.

The person in charge

You decide, the agents carry out.

The agents propose, prepare and check; you review and publish with GitHub Desktop. Publishing credentials stay on your computer, and every change is recorded in the history of the website.

SEO specialist

The course

A website of your own, updated by you, at almost no cost.

The course teaches how to set up, in Claude Cowork, a group of agents that builds the website, updates it and positions it according to written rules. The site is static, that is, made of ready-made pages, and is published on GitHub Pages: by the end of the first session it is already online.

Savings. Hosting and statistics are free; a new page or an article needs no paid outside work. What remains is the Claude Pro subscription and, if you wish, the domain.

Speed. A change is requested and goes live within the same hour.

Precision. The change is described by the person who knows the business, and reaches the website exactly as it was intended.

Who it is for. Owners, professionals and communication managers. No programming experience is required.

Duration

4 hours, in two two-hour sessions.

Format

Online only, at least 5 people, including from different organisations.

Tools

Claude Pro, which includes Claude Cowork, with the desktop app; GitHub Desktop; free GitHub, Google and GoatCounter accounts. In longer working sessions the plan's usage limit may be reached; it resets after a few hours, and the course shows how to organise the work to stay within it.

Fee

€100 per person, VAT excluded. Paid in advance. All fees.

The programme

Two sessions, each with a result ready for use.

First session · Brand, website and publication

Working with agents and the four roles. From the company brief to the brand book, then the static website, generated on your own computer and verified by automatic checks before publication on GitHub Pages.

  • Brand book: colours, typefaces, mark and tone of voice
  • Static website, automatic checks and publication
  • Result: The published website, the brand book and the role instructions

Second session · Search engines and blog

Search Console and cookie-free statistics, keywords for each page, structured data and social previews. A map linking keywords, services and articles, a first article and a scheduled periodic check.

  • Technical SEO, structured data and social previews
  • Editorial plan and the first blog article
  • Result: The first article published and the periodic check running

Between the two sessions

About half an hour, with the step-by-step guide, so that the second session works on real data.

  • Search Console verified and sitemap submitted
  • GoatCounter running
  • Domain connected, if planned
  • A first change made independently

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What do I keep at the end of the course?

The published website, on your own domain if you have one; the brand book; the four skills and the instruction file in your account; SEO in place with Search Console; GoatCounter statistics; the editorial plan and the first article. Everything stays on your own accounts.

Is Claude Pro enough?

Yes, for the course and for the everyday running of the website. On the most intensive working days the plan's usage limit may be reached; it resets after a few hours, and in the sessions we show how to organise requests to make the most of it.

How much does the website cost to run after the course?

Hosting and statistics are free; what remains is the Claude Pro subscription and, if you wish, a domain of your own. Changes, new pages and articles are handled by you, with the agents set up during the course.

I already have a website: can I rebuild it during the course?

The course builds a new website. Migrating existing content is available as a tailored course.

Can I build an online shop?

No: the GitHub Pages terms do not allow it. The course covers presentation websites and blogs.

How do I enrol?

By writing to info@ggtechnologies.sm with the number of participants. Enrolment is completed by paying in advance; courses start with at least 5 people.
