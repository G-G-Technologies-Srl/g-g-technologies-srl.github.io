The course

Where the data goes, and which law applies.

Choosing a model is not only about the quality of the answers. What also matters is the jurisdiction the data answers to, the licence, and whether the model can run inside the organisation.

Who it is for. Those who decide on tools and infrastructure in a company or a professional practice.

Duration

4 hours, in two two-hour sessions.

Format

Online or on site, at least 5 people.

Tools

Claude Pro or ChatGPT Plus; LM Studio or Ollama, installed before the second session. A computer with at least 16 GB of memory is recommended.

Fee

From €100 per person, VAT excluded; on site plus €800. Paid in advance. All fees.

Further reading

The programme

Two sessions, each with a result ready for use.

First session · The model landscape

The same task given to American services (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini), Chinese ones (DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi) and European ones (Mistral).

  • Data jurisdiction and licence terms
  • Cloud services and open-weight models you can download and install
  • Result: A comparison table and the criteria for updating it

Second session · Local AI

Running a model on your own computer with LM Studio or Ollama, both free. Tests with Llama, Gemma, Qwen, Mistral and gpt-oss.

  • Performance, limits and memory requirements
  • Hybrid set-up with masking of personal data
  • Result: A reasoned choice between cloud, local and hybrid

At the end

The materials produced in the two sessions are exported to the participant's own files, which open in any program and can be used independently.

  • A comparison table and the criteria for updating it
  • A reasoned choice between cloud, local and hybrid

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I attend on a company computer?

Yes, if installing LM Studio or Ollama is allowed. It is advisable to check this beforehand with the IT manager.

Are the models the same in every edition?

No. The landscape changes quickly, and the list is updated before each edition. The selection criteria stay the same.

How do I enrol?

By writing to info@ggtechnologies.sm with the format and the number of participants. Enrolment is completed by paying in advance; courses start with at least 5 people.
