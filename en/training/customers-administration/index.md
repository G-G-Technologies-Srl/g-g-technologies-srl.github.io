*The course*

## Recurring tasks are where an assistant is most useful.

Summarising correspondence, preparing meetings, checking invoices and deadlines: tasks that come back every week, and that a well-configured assistant carries out consistently.

**Who it is for.** Sales, administration and secretarial offices.

#### Duration

4 hours, in two two-hour sessions.

#### Format

Online or on site, at least 5 people.

#### Tools

Claude Pro or ChatGPT Plus. The course runs entirely in the browser.

#### Fee

From €100 per person, VAT excluded; on site plus €800. Paid in advance. [All fees](https://ggtechnologies.sm/en/training/#fees).

*The programme*

## Two sessions, each with a result ready for use.

### First session · Customers and negotiations

A customer record built from the documentation, summaries of correspondence, meeting preparation.

- Recurring replies to customers
- An assistant configured on the customer base
- **Result:** A collection of requests for customer management

### Second session · Administration

Invoices, deadlines, expense claims and reconciliations: recurring tasks, from the document to the check.

- Extracting data from accounting documents
- Checks and reconciliations
- **Result:** A complete procedure for a recurring task

### At the end

The materials produced in the two sessions are exported to the participant's own files, which open in any program and can be used independently.

- A collection of requests for customer management
- A complete procedure for a recurring task
