*Course · Foundations*

Rules for the proper use of AI assistants, how to write requests, and how to configure a dedicated assistant. It is the foundation course of the catalogue.

*The course*

## A shared rule, before the tools.

AI assistants are already in companies, often used by each person in their own way. The course sets a shared rule: what may be uploaded, how a request is written, how an answer is checked.

**Who it is for.** Owners, professionals and department heads introducing artificial intelligence into the organisation with shared rules.

#### Duration

4 hours, in two two-hour sessions.

#### Format

Online or on site, at least 5 people.

#### Tools

Claude Pro or ChatGPT Plus. The course runs entirely in the browser.

#### Fee

From €100 per person, VAT excluded; on site plus €800. Paid in advance. [All fees](https://ggtechnologies.sm/en/training/#fees).

*The programme*

## Two sessions, each with a result ready for use.

### First session · How it works, and data

How AI assistants operate and where they are most useful. The structure of an effective request: role, context, task and format.

- Personal and business plans, where uploaded data goes
- Obligations under the GDPR and the AI Act
- **Result:** Two verified requests and a sheet for building new ones

### Second session · The dedicated assistant

Configuring a project with instructions, sector, tone and reference documents. How to recognise wrong answers.

- Standing instructions and reference documents
- Answers that cannot be verified: how to spot them
- **Result:** A configured assistant and a checklist for daily use

### At the end

The materials produced in the two sessions are exported to the participant's own files, which open in any program and can be used independently.

- Two verified requests and a sheet for building new ones
- A configured assistant and a checklist for daily use
- The course materials

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to have used an AI assistant before?

No. The course starts from how the tools work and is also designed for those introducing them into the organisation now.

### Does the course replace legal advice?

No. It sets out the main rules and obligations; assessing a specific data processing activity remains with the controller and its advisers.
