The course

The assistant reads and compares. Signing stays with the accountable person.

A contract spreads obligations, deadlines and risks over many pages. The course teaches how to extract them, compare them with the organisation's own terms and prepare a draft for review.

Who it is for. Owners, purchasing and sales offices, professional practices.

Duration

4 hours, in two two-hour sessions.

Format

Online or on site, at least 5 people.

Tools

Claude Pro or ChatGPT Plus. The course runs entirely in the browser.

Fee

From €100 per person, VAT excluded; on site plus €800. Paid in advance. All fees.

The programme

Two sessions, each with a result ready for use.

First session · Analysing a contract

Identifying clauses, deadlines, obligations and risks, and comparing them with the organisation's standard terms.

  • A reusable analysis sheet
  • A project holding the standard terms
  • Result: An analysis sheet and a configured project

Second session · Drafting and comparing

A draft from a template, comparing versions, preparing a negotiation.

  • Comparing two versions of an agreement
  • Limits of the tool and responsibility
  • Result: A drafting and review procedure

At the end

The materials produced in the two sessions are exported to the participant's own files, which open in any program and can be used independently.

  • An analysis sheet and a configured project
  • A drafting and review procedure
  • The course materials

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the course only for lawyers?

No. It is also designed for people who negotiate and manage contracts without legal training.

Can I leave the drafting to the assistant?

The assistant prepares the draft and the comparison; review and signature remain with the person responsible.

How do I enrol?

By writing to info@ggtechnologies.sm with the format and the number of participants. Enrolment is completed by paying in advance; courses start with at least 5 people.
