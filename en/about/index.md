*What we do*

## AI analyses and proposes. A person checks and decides.

We design and build artificial intelligence solutions for companies and professional firms, and we see their adoption through from start to finish: training the people, choosing the architecture, development, integration into processes and compliance with the rules.

*The first step*

### Training, before any project.

Every project begins with the people who will use the tools. The courses are practical, with a fixed programme, for management, professionals and individual departments: professional use of AI, local AI models, software development, production and quality, customers and administration, contracts, website and SEO with an AI agent.

Each course lasts four hours over two sessions, online or on site; the website course is online only. The programme is documented, and that is the evidence of the measures Article 4 of the AI Act asks of companies: building AI skills in the people who use it.

[The course catalogue →](https://ggtechnologies.sm/en/training/)

### AI for software development

AI agents that write code, brought into your team's development process, on the models and infrastructure you choose. Costs are estimated before the investment.

### Local AI and compliance

Models run on your own workstations or servers, or in a hybrid architecture that masks personal data before anything is sent outside. Every project is set up against the rules that apply to it: the GDPR, the AI Act, Italy's AI law (132/2025) and, for high-risk systems, Legislative Decree 160/2026. [How it works](https://ggtechnologies.sm/en/services/on-premise-ai/).

### AI for security

Agents with a defined scope of action, access limited to what is strictly needed, and a person's approval for sensitive operations. We also review the AI tools already in use in your company. [The reasoning, in writing](https://ggtechnologies.sm/en/insights/autonomous-agents-boundaries/).

### AI for process automation

Repetitive work moves to AI, integrated into the management system (ERP), the other software in use and email. Results are measured against indicators agreed before work starts.

### AI for data and document analysis

Your company archive becomes something you can question: every answer cites the document it comes from. We extract data from contracts, invoices and technical documents.

### Advice to management

We work alongside management on priorities, investments, the choice of suppliers and the rules for how your company uses AI. Then we build what has been decided.

## From factory systems to university research. In chronological order.

01

### 1997 – 2002 · Silcea Group, then SkyLab

Head of information systems, tasked with putting the new ERP into production. Then the founding of SkyLab, which assembled workstations for architecture practices, with the graphics cards chosen to run CAD.

02

### 2002 – 2020 · Eighteen years at Gruppo Fontanot

Project manager and software architect, then CIO, then head of development and digital innovation manager for the group. Production processes, logistics, sales network, configurators. *FAQ*

## Frequently asked questions

### How many of you are there?

The core is small and the network around it is wide: collaborators and partners chosen for the project, across several countries. That is why the person who answers you is the one who designs, with no layers in between: the person you talk to is the same one who builds the system.

### Do you only work in San Marino?

No. The office is in Serravalle, clients are in Italy too, and the collaborators are spread across several countries. The software is designed and developed in Europe.

### Is San Marino in the European Union?

San Marino is an independent state, outside the European Union. The practical question for your data is where it gets processed and who answers for it: in an on-premise installation the processing happens on your own servers, in your own country, and the one who answers for it is you — you can verify that from your own network traffic. Where a service of ours processes data on your behalf, the applicable law and the place of processing are fixed in the contract, before anything starts.

### Who answers if I write?

The person who does the designing. The reply comes, within one working day, from the same person who signs the code, the quote and the scientific papers. The first conversation is about whether we are the right people for your problem: if we are not, saying so quickly is faster for everyone.

### Why are there no client logos?

Because our work sits under confidentiality agreements and we keep them: a firm that shows you other people's names will one day show yours. It is the same choice we propose for your data. What can be checked from outside is on this page, and we will gladly talk about projects by describing the problems, not the names.
