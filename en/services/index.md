# Electronics, robotics and artificial intelligence, under one roof.

*Services*

We design and build wearable devices, industrial automation and artificial intelligence systems. Every service rests on the DigiSense® framework, so a project starts from an existing base.

*The services*

## From the sensor to the model, under a single responsibility.

The work starts from the process: where time is lost, where mistakes happen, where a piece of data already exists but nobody reads it. The technology is chosen afterwards.

Medical wearables and remote monitoring, industrial robotics and automation, AI consulting and development, on-premise AI, agent security and AI for software development are six ways of applying the same expertise. On one project they can work together.

We are based in San Marino. The services cover San Marino, Italy and the rest of Europe.

### [Medical wearables](https://ggtechnologies.sm/en/services/medical-wearables/)

Board, firmware and remote-monitoring platform, in a single project.

### [Robotics & Automation](https://ggtechnologies.sm/en/services/robotics-automation/)

Automation at the machine, integration with your systems, downtime measured.

### [Artificial Intelligence](https://ggtechnologies.sm/en/services/artificial-intelligence/)

Agents that read documents and query your systems, with the measure agreed first.

### [On-premise AI](https://ggtechnologies.sm/en/services/on-premise-ai/)

How to choose between fully local, hybrid and cloud, starting from your data.

### [AI for security](https://ggtechnologies.sm/en/services/ai-security/)

Agents with a defined scope of action, and a review of the ones already running.

### [AI for software development](https://ggtechnologies.sm/en/services/ai-software-development/)

Agents writing code inside your process, with the costs estimated beforehand.
